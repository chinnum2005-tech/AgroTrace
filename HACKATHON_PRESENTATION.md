## Q&A Preparation

### Common Questions & Answers:

**Q: How do you verify the data is accurate?**
A: "Great question! We use multiple verification layers:
1. GPS coordinates automatically captured
2. Timestamps from blockchain
3. Actor identity through JWT auth
4. Cross-referencing with IoT sensors (optional)
5. Third-party audits for certifications"

**Q: What prevents someone from entering fake data?**
A: "Three things:
1. **Blockchain immutability** - once recorded, can't be changed
2. **Reputation system** - actors build trust scores over time
3. **Audit trails** - random inspections can verify claims
4. **Economic incentives** - fraud damages reputation permanently"

**Q: How do you scale this to millions of products?**
A: "We've designed for scale:
1. Database indexing on all query fields
2. Caching layer for frequently accessed data
3. CDN for static assets
4. Microservices architecture
5. Can move to Layer 2 blockchain for lower fees"

**Q: What's your go-to-market strategy?**
A: "Three phases:
1. **Phase 1**: Partner with local organic farms (prove concept)
2. **Phase 2**: Expand to regional distributors (scale vertically)
3. **Phase 3**: License to enterprise retailers (scale horizontally)"

**Q: How do you make money from small farmers?**
A: "Tiered pricing:
- **Free tier**: Up to 10 products/month (get them hooked)
- **Starter**: $29/month for 100 products
- **Growth**: $99/month for unlimited
Most will upgrade as they see consumer demand"

## Pro Tips for Winning

### During Demo:
1. **Show, Don't Tell** - Let the product speak
2. **React Genuinely** - Be amazed by your own creation
3. **Pause for Effect** - Let judges absorb wow moments
4. **Make Eye Contact** - Connect with judges
5. **Use Hands Naturally** - Point to screen elements
6. **Vary Your Voice** - Emphasize key points
7. **Smile** - Show you're excited about this

### Body Language:
- Stand tall, shoulders back
- Open gestures (no crossed arms)
- Move with purpose (don't pace nervously)
- Face the judges, not the screen
- Use the whole space

### Voice Tone:
- Start confident and clear
- Build energy during demo
- Slow down for important points
- Pause before big reveals
- End strong and enthusiastic
